# SEO and Content Architecture

## Commercial clusters

- **Tender writing:** care tender writer, health and social care tender writing, bid writing services, social care bid consultant.
- **Decision and readiness:** bid or no-bid decision, tender readiness, can a new care provider win contracts, tender qualification.
- **Quality and review:** tender review, bid review, improve tender score, why care tenders fail.
- **Training and capability:** tender training, bid writing training for care providers, in-house bid-team coaching.
- **Care settings:** domiciliary care, supported living, residential and nursing, children’s residential and supported accommodation, complex care, community health and related legitimate service intents.
- **Opportunity discovery:** live care tenders, council care contracts, NHS opportunities and defined tender categories.

## Page-to-intent rules

1. One authoritative page serves each materially distinct intent.
2. Service-setting pages must contain setting-specific buyer concerns and evidence, not substituted labels.
3. Location-only pages are prohibited unless they satisfy a genuine distinct user need with substantive local evidence.
4. Expired tender pages remain useful only where they preserve official facts and connect to current relevant opportunities; otherwise they are excluded or retired deliberately.
5. Blog articles answer a clear buyer question and link to one relevant commercial route without forcing a sales pitch.
6. Titles, H1s and descriptions prioritise clarity; keyword variants appear naturally in supporting copy.

## Internal-link model

Homepage → service comparison → individual service → relevant care settings, proof and contact.

Care setting → relevant live tenders, case study, article and service.

Tender detail → qualification method, matching service and prefilled enquiry.

Blog article → related guide/service/tender and two useful neighbouring articles.

Case study → relevant care setting, service and contact route.

No indexable page may be orphaned or more than three meaningful clicks from a primary hub.

## Technical rules

- Self-referencing canonical on indexable pages.
- Preview environments use `noindex, nofollow`; production uses page-level intent.
- Stable XML sitemap groups content and excludes API, private, search-state and duplicate routes.
- Descriptive 301 redirects preserve valuable legacy URLs.
- Organisation, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Service and Article schema are used only where accurate and visible.
- FAQ markup is not added merely for rich-result expectations.
- Images use descriptive filenames, useful alt text and responsive dimensions.
